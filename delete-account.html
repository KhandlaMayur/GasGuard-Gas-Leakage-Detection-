Delete Your GasGuard Account

GasGuard allows users to request the deletion of their account and associated personal data.

Important: Account deletion may permanently remove your account and associated GasGuard data. This action may not be reversible.

How to Delete Your Account

  1. Open the GasGuard application.
  2. Log in using your registered email address and password.
  3. Go to the Profile section.
  4. Select Delete Account.
  5. Confirm your account deletion request.

What Data May Be Deleted?

When your account deletion request is processed, the following information associated with your GasGuard account may be deleted:

  • User profile information
  • Name and email address associated with the account
  • Registered gas monitoring device information
  • Device configuration and threshold settings
  • Gas monitoring readings and history
  • Alert history associated with your account
  • Other application data associated with your user account

Request Account Deletion by Email

If you are unable to access the GasGuard application, you can request account deletion by contacting us through email.

Please include the following information:

  • Your registered email address
  • Your request to delete your GasGuard account

Data Retention

After an account deletion request is processed, account-related data will be deleted unless we are required to retain certain information for legal, security, fraud prevention, or other legitimate purposes.
