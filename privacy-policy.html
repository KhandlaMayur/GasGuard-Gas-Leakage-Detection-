Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

GasGuard ("we", "our", or "us") provides a gas leakage monitoring and safety alert application.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, stored, and protected when you use the GasGuard mobile application.

By using GasGuard, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

A. Account Information

When you create an account, we may collect:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • User ID generated by Firebase Authentication

This information is used to create and manage your GasGuard account.

B. Device Information

When you add a gas monitoring device to the application, we may store:

  • Device ID
  • Device name
  • Device location
  • Device owner information
  • Device status
  • Device online/offline status

C. Gas Sensor Data

GasGuard collects gas monitoring information transmitted by connected gas detection hardware.

  • Current gas level
  • Gas sensor readings
  • Sensor status
  • Safety status
  • Configured gas threshold
  • Warning and danger status
  • Reading timestamps

D. Alert Information

GasGuard may store safety alert information including:

  • Alert ID
  • Device ID
  • Alert severity
  • Gas level at the time of the alert
  • Alert status
  • Alert acknowledgement status
  • Alert acknowledgement time
  • Alert expiration information

3. How We Use Your Information

GasGuard uses collected information to:

  • Create and manage user accounts
  • Authenticate users
  • Connect users with registered gas monitoring devices
  • Display real-time gas sensor readings
  • Calculate gas safety status
  • Allow users to configure gas leakage thresholds
  • Generate WARNING and DANGER alerts
  • Send safety notifications
  • Maintain alert history
  • Display gas reading history and analytics
  • Improve application functionality and reliability
GasGuard does not sell personal information for advertising purposes.

4. Firebase Services

GasGuard uses Firebase services provided by Google to support core application functionality.

Firebase services used by the application may include:

  • Firebase Authentication
  • Firebase Realtime Database
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging or notification services
  • Firebase Analytics, if enabled

Firebase services may process information according to Google's privacy practices.

You can review Google's Privacy Policy here: Google Privacy Policy

6. Data Sharing

GasGuard does not sell your personal information.

We do not intentionally share your personal information with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

Data may be processed by service providers necessary for operating the application, including Firebase services provided by Google.

7. Notifications

GasGuard may send notifications related to gas safety events.

  • Gas warning alerts
  • Gas danger alerts
  • Threshold-related safety alerts
  • Alert acknowledgement reminders

Notifications are intended to provide important safety information related to registered gas monitoring devices.

Users can manage notification permissions through their Android device settings.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect user information and application data.

Security measures may include:

  • Firebase Authentication
  • Firebase Security Rules
  • User-specific database access controls
  • Secure communication with cloud services
Important: No method of electronic storage or transmission over the internet can guarantee complete security.

9. Data Retention

GasGuard retains user and device information for as long as necessary to provide application functionality.

Information that may be retained includes:

  • Account information
  • Registered device information
  • Gas sensor readings
  • Alert history
  • Safety analytics

10. Account and Data Deletion

Users may request deletion of their GasGuard account and associated data.

When account deletion is completed, associated information may be deleted, including:

  • User profile information
  • Registered device information associated with the user
  • Gas reading history associated with the user
  • Alert history associated with the user
  • Firebase Authentication account credentials
Account Deletion: Users can request account deletion through the Account Settings section of the GasGuard mobile application.

11. Children's Privacy

GasGuard is not specifically designed for children.

The application is intended for users who operate or monitor gas detection and safety devices.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate authorization.

12. Third-Party Services

GasGuard may use third-party services necessary for application functionality.

These services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when application features or data practices change.

Any updates will be reflected by changing the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page.

Users are encouraged to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
